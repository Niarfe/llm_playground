VISITORS' BOOK -- WEEK OF THE 14th

  12th   Reverend Pike ......... Afternoon tea. Departed before six.
  13th   Lady Harrington ....... DEPARTED for London by the evening train.
                                 Not expected back before the 20th.
  14th   Miss Verity ........... Arrived at six o'clock. Stayed the night.
  14th   Dr Fenwick ............ Called upon the Colonel. Left before dinner.
  15th   (no callers)
