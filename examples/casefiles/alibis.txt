STATEMENTS TAKEN -- EVENING OF THE 14th

Mrs Danvers ....... In the kitchen from eight until ten, with Cook and the
                    scullery maid. Both confirm without hesitation.

Reverend Pike ..... At the vestry meeting until half past nine. Six members
                    of the parish council confirm his presence throughout.

Colonel Ashby ..... States he was walking alone in the rose garden. No person
                    saw him. No lamp was lit in the garden that evening.

Miss Verity ....... In the conservatory with Lady Harrington's maid until
                    nine o'clock. The maid confirms.

Thomas Beel ....... Serving at table until half past eight, then in the
                    servants' hall. Cook confirms.
