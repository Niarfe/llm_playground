ESTATE MAINTENANCE LOG

  3rd    Gutter cleared, east wing. Ladder returned to the stables.
  9th    WINE CELLAR LOCK REPLACED, the old one having seized.
         One key only was cut, and issued to Thomas Beel, under-butler.
         All previous cellar keys no longer fit the door and were collected
         and destroyed.
  11th   Glasshouse pane replaced, north side.
  13th   Carriage clock in the drawing room regulated.
